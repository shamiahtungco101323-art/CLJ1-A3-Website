FIRST PRELIM — Historical Development and Principles of Criminal Justice

1. Concept of Crime and Justice

What comes first, the law or the crime?

  • Law is that which is laid down, established or decreed.
  • Most laws are penal in nature, meaning they define crime, treat of their nature, and provide for their punishment.
  • Nullum crimen nulla poena
