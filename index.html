03 / JURISDICTION

Know the
court's authority.

Jurisdiction is the power or authority given by law to a court to hear and decide a particular case.

01

Subject Matter

The authority of a court over the type or nature of a case.

02

Territorial

The authority of a court over cases connected to a particular geographical area.

03

Personal

The authority of a court over the persons involved in a case.

CLASSIFICATIONS

Understanding Jurisdiction

Original Jurisdiction 01

The power of a court to hear and decide a case at the first instance.

Appellate Jurisdiction 02

The power of a higher court to review decisions made by a lower court.

Exclusive Jurisdiction 03

A case may be heard only by the court specifically authorized by law.

Concurrent Jurisdiction 04

More than one court may have authority over a particular case.

04 / PROCEEDINGS

From accusation
to decision.

A criminal case passes through several stages. Each stage has a specific purpose in protecting due process and administering justice.

01 / ARRAIGNMENT

The accused is informed.

The accused is formally informed of the charge and is asked to enter a plea.

02 / PRE-TRIAL

The case is prepared.

The parties and the court address matters that can help prepare the case for trial.

03 / TRIAL

Evidence is presented.

The prosecution and defense present their evidence and arguments according to the rules of procedure.

04 / JUDGMENT

The court decides.

After considering the evidence, the court issues its judgment.

05 / APPEAL

A decision may be reviewed.

When allowed by law, a party may seek review by a higher court.

06 / DECISION

The case moves forward.

The court's decision is handled according to applicable rules and procedures.

!
Search Warrant Reminder

Search warrants must comply with constitutional and legal requirements. Their issuance is subject to the rules provided by law.

05 / JUDGMENT

When the court
reaches a verdict.

After evaluating the evidence and applying the law, the court may issue a judgment of conviction or acquittal.

✓
OUTCOME 01

Conviction

A judgment of conviction means that the court finds the accused guilty based on the required legal standard.

○
OUTCOME 02

Acquittal

An acquittal means that the accused is found not guilty under the applicable law and evidence.
